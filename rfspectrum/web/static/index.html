    Band plan bar: green = short-range devices, blue = ISM, purple = licensed, red = take care. Labels summarise Ofcom IR 2030 / ERC Rec 70-03 for training only.

    EMCON score: 100 means nothing heard. Each emitter costs 15 points and the busiest one's duty cycle costs up to 50, over the last 60 s. Baselined emitters are not scored. Anyone with a cheap receiver can see what this screen sees.

    Detection: power above the noise floor, held for a time, inside a band. Drone links are flagged from a hopping control link plus a video link. Bearing is unknown: confirm by sight, sound or radar.
